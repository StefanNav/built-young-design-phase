How to spot AI slop

  • It looks like every other app
  • Hype words that say nothing
  • Too many features on one screen
  • Fake people, stats or reviews
  • Effects instead of ideas

Good UX in 9 rules

Good UX means people can use your app without help.

1
Obvious next step
2
Speaks the user's language
3
Shows what's happening
4
Easy to undo
5
Prevents mistakes, and helps fix them
6
Consistent and familiar
7
Shows, doesn't make you remember
8
Only what's needed
9
Works for everyone
Don't explain all nine. Pick two or three and give a quick example. The student checks every wireframe against these, and my comments name the rule.
