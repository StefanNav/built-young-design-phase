Nobody builds the whole app at once. Epics split it into pieces. The core loop comes first, because nothing else matters if it doesn't work. Each epic depends on the ones before it and unlocks the next: that's the Depends on and Unlocks columns in the Epics doc. Next, what that looks like for Workout Log.

2
